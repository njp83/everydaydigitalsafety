Digital Safety Australia

Helping families stay safe and private online

Simple, independent guidance on online privacy, apps, and AI — written for kids, parents, and educators.

The internet changes fast, and it’s not always clear how new apps, games, and AI tools affect children’s privacy. This site exists to explain those changes clearly, without fear-mongering, tracking, or technical jargon.

Our mission

To explain online privacy in a calm, honest way — so families, schools, and young people can make informed choices about the technology they use.

  • Understandable
  • Age-appropriate
  • Independent
  • Respectful of people’s data

This site is for

  • Parents trying to understand apps, social media, and AI tools
  • Teachers and schools looking for clear, neutral explanations
  • Kids and teens who want to learn how the internet works
  • Anyone who cares about privacy but isn’t technical

What we cover

  • Plain-English explanations of popular apps and platforms
  • How AI tools collect and use data
  • Online privacy basics for kids and teens
  • Guidance for families and schools
